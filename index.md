## 🛠 Projects & Software

### [Canary](https://github.com/anb94/Canary)
**Published in BMC Bioinformatics.** A singularity-based tool for the semi-automated conversion of MaCH files to PLINK files for GWAS analysis.

### [Minimal RNA-Seq Tutorial](https://github.com/anb94/minimal-rna-seq-tutorial)
**Educational Resource.** A streamlined workflow designed for new students. It covers gene-level exploratory analysis and differential expression on lab servers.
